Before and after training

Before: Have a familiar snack or meal with carbohydrates, such as banana and bread, or rice and egg. Allow time to digest.

After: Have a regular meal with carbohydrates and protein, such as rice, fish, and vegetables, or rice and chicken.

Daily: Eat regular meals and snacks. Include different food groups. Do not skip meals to change your body shape.
