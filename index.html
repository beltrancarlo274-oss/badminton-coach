Technique guide

Simple coaching cues

Ready position

Keep your feet about shoulder-width apart. Bend your knees slightly. Hold the racket in front. Stay relaxed and balanced.

Split step

Use a small, balanced hop as your opponent contacts the shuttle. Land lightly, then push toward the shot.

Overhead clear and smash

Turn your body sideways, prepare the racket early and contact the shuttle high and in front. Rotate smoothly. Recover to your base after the shot. Prioritize control before power.

Net shot

Move in with a controlled lunge. Keep your racket head up. Use a relaxed grip and a small touch. Push back to a balanced position.

Defense

Stay low with the racket in front. Watch the opponent's contact point. Use short steps and guide the racket through the shuttle.

Serve practice

Use a repeatable setup. Aim for a small target area. Practice legal, consistent serves and check the rules used in your competition.

Training safety

Warm up before fast movement. Use a clear, non-slip court area and suitable shoes. Take water breaks. Leave hard smash practice until your shoulder and technique are ready.
